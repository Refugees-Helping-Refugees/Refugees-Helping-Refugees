Social Care Network services

RHR is part of the Finger Lakes Social Care Network, WeLinkCare by Forward Leading IPA (FLIPA). Our team can help you understand the support you may qualify for.

Coordinated referrals

Gain connections to community resources and support.

Rent and utility payment assistance

Help exploring available assistance based on eligibility.

Advocacy and outreach

Support finding resources & understanding next steps.

Case management support

Help navigating services and coordinating support.

Eligibility is assessed through screening. Learn more about WeLinkCare by FLIPA →

Our mission

Refugees Helping Refugees is a refugee-led organization in Rochester focused on promoting growth, self-determination, and self-reliance among refugees. We offer education, training, and support to help refugees build on their strengths and succeed.

Our programs and services

  • Workforce Development Training
  • ESOL Classes
  • Case Management
  • Immigration and Legal Services
  • Mental Health Services
  • Social Care Network

About us

2002 • Sadiya founded the organization to support the Somali Community of Western New York.

2021 • The organization expanded to support refugees of all nationalities.
